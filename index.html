Why it's different

Built with hard limits, on purpose

Never diagnoses

It explains general medical concepts — it never tells you what's wrong with you.

Never contradicts your team

Every answer defers to the doctors and nurses actually treating you.

Written to calm, not alarm

Sixth-grade reading level, no jargon, no scary language — and no false reassurance either.

ER Companion is an educational tool, not a diagnostic one. It does not replace your doctors and nurses, and it cannot assess your personal medical situation. If something feels urgent, tell a staff member right away.
